Before generating stories, scenes, or videos, you MUST read and follow:

### Key Channel Rules:
1. **Funny 3D Slapstick Only**: Focus on hilarious, physical comedy, dance mishaps, and funny character interactions (inspired by viral baby & duck dance shorts). NO slow educational, bedtime, or counting videos.
2. **Real Character Animation**: Never generate static image camera zoompans. Always use `engine: 'full_ai_video'` (Kling Image-to-Video via Fal.ai) so characters have authentic movement, dancing, and cartoon physics.
3. **Format**: 9:16 vertical, 12–18 seconds total, punchy yellow cartoon subtitles, bouncy comedic BGM.
